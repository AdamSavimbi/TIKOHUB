# TikoPoa

TikoPoa is a Kenyan event ticketing platform: organizers sell tickets, attendees pay with M-Pesa, and tickets open in the browser.

## Language

### People

**Organizer**:
A person or group that creates an event and sells tickets for it.
_Avoid_: Host, promoter, vendor

**Attendee**:
A person who buys or holds a ticket to an event.
_Avoid_: Buyer, guest, customer, fan

### Ticketing

**On-sale**:
The moment tickets for an event become available to buy.
_Avoid_: Launch, release, drop

### Landing page

**Header pill**:
The rounded navigation bar shown at the top of every page.
_Avoid_: Navbar, pill bar, menu bar

**Live pulse**:
The landing-page section that shows live site activity and the countdown to the next on-sale.
_Avoid_: Live chat, activity feed, ticker

**Lineup**:
The moving row of event cards in the hero, labelled "Top picks".
_Avoid_: Carousel, marquee, poster strip, slider

**Selected event**:
The one event in the lineup whose details and category are shown in the hero.
_Avoid_: Active event, featured event, current event
